What happens at a consultation?

Your consultation is a conversation about your goals, medical history, and the options available to you. It is the time to ask about suitability, risks, recovery, anticipated outcomes, and costs.

What if I’m not sure which procedure I want?

Start with your goals. The team can help you explore surgical and non-surgical options and discuss which, if any, may be appropriate for you.

Can I see before-and-after photographs?

Yes. Selected cases are in our patient gallery. Other photographs are shown only in the office to respect patient privacy. Ask to review examples relevant to your goals.

How do I ask about financing?

Contact the practice to discuss current payment and financing arrangements. You can also visit the practice’s financing page for more information.
