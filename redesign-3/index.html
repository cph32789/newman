THE NEXT STEP IS A CONVERSATION.

BE CURIOUS.
ASK ANYTHING.

What happens at a consultation?

A personal conversation about your goals, medical history, options, suitability, recovery, risks, realistic expectations, and costs. Bring your questions. Take the time you need.

What if I don’t know which procedure I want?

Start with what matters to you. Dr. Newman and the team can help you explore the options and discuss whether a surgical or non-surgical approach may be appropriate.

Where can I view patient results?

Selected cases are in our patient gallery. Some photographs are shown only in the office to protect patient privacy. Ask to see examples relevant to your goals.

How do I discuss payment and financing?

Contact the practice for current arrangements, or visit the insurance and financing page.
