Try the Admin.

The Admin is where you change the website yourselves, with no code. It is just as important as the site itself, so please try it.

  1. Open the Admin and sign in. You will be given a separate Admin password.
  2. Go to Pages & images, choose any page, and press Edit this page. Click any wording to change it, move or hide whole sections, and replace pictures. Then press Save changes.
  3. Go to Team. Change a title, or click a photo to choose what stays in view, then press Save changes.
  4. Go to Procedures, choose a procedure, edit a sentence, and press Save changes.
  5. Go to Reviews and add a review, or Media and add a YouTube video by pasting its link.
  6. Go to Offers and add a test offer with an end date, or Skin care to edit a brand.
  7. Go to Gallery. Try hiding a case, changing the order, or turning 18+ on or off for a gallery.
  8. Press Publish at the top. Your changes become visible to visitors, and the pages are rebuilt.
  9. Open the website in a private window to see what a visitor sees. Then change it back if you like.

Good to know: changes stay as drafts until you press Publish, so nobody sees half-finished work. Anything you try here can be undone.

What to look for.

A quick checklist. Make notes as you go.

  • Does it feel like the practice: calm, confident, high-end? Does it feel like an app on your phone?
  • Are the fonts, colors and spacing what you want? Anything too big, too small or too busy?
  • Is every name, title, address, phone number and fact correct?
  • Is the wording right for Dr. Newman? Anything that sounds like a promise, or that he would not say?
  • Are the photos the right ones? Which should be replaced with better ones?
  • Can you find what a new patient would look for within a few taps?
  • Is there anything missing that the old website had, or that you want to add?
