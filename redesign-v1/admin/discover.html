Discover feed.

The scrolling cards on the home page, and the posts in Follow along. Add, edit, reorder, hide or delete them. Keep it useful and calm: no promises and no medical claims.

Card types. Procedure spotlights one procedure. Insight is a short helpful read. Guide starts from a goal. Patient voice is a quote shared with permission. Social post shows one of the posts from the other tab. “Show under” picks the filter chips the card appears under on the home page.

These posts appear in Follow along and can also be used as feed cards. Each links to a procedure page. The Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Pinterest addresses are in Settings.

Changes you save are drafts. They go live when you press Publish, the gold button at the top of the page (it stays in view as you scroll).
