Procedures & categories.

Add and edit every procedure and treatment: its name, description, recovery and questions. Organise them into categories. Each procedure gets its own page, search entry and share card when you publish.

Categories

The groups visitors browse by. Rename them, change the order, hide one, or add a new one. A category can be deleted only when it has no procedures.

New categories appear right away in the Gallery admin and the Results gallery. When published, they also get a card on the home page and a tab in the procedure explorer.

Changes you save are drafts. They go live when you press Publish, the gold button at the top of the page (it stays in view as you scroll). Publishing rebuilds the procedure pages.
