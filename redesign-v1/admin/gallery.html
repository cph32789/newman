The Gallery.

Manage every before-and-after gallery in one place. Add, rename, hide or delete a gallery. Decide whether it asks visitors to confirm they are 18 or older. Inside each gallery, switch cases on or off, drag them into order, add new ones and correct any text.

Changes you save are drafts. They go live on the Results gallery when you press Publish, the gold button at the top of the page. Case details were copied from the current website; please check each one and mark it Checked.
