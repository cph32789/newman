Common questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Questions about one procedure are answered on that procedure’s page.

What happens at a consultation?

Your consultation is a conversation about your goals, medical history and the options available to you. It is the time to ask about suitability, risks, recovery, anticipated outcomes and costs.

What if I’m not sure which procedure I want?

Start with your goals. Dr. Newman and the team can help you explore surgical and non-surgical options and discuss which, if any, may be appropriate for you.

What are the risks?

Every procedure carries some uncertainty and risk. Dr. Newman will review the risks that apply to you in detail before you decide, and you can reduce them by following your care team’s advice closely.

Can I see before-and-after photographs?

Yes. Selected cases appear on the matching procedure page, and in our patient gallery, when patients have given permission. Other photographs are shown only in the office, to protect patient privacy.

Is plastic surgery covered by insurance?

Cosmetic surgery, which reshapes normal structures to improve appearance, is usually not covered by insurance. Reconstructive surgery, which corrects abnormal structures caused by birth defects, injury, infection, tumors or disease, may be. Your policy is a contract between you and your plan, so please check with your provider. If you have an HMO plan, a referral from your primary care physician is required before your appointment.

How do I pay?

We accept all major credit cards and CareCredit (restrictions apply). Please contact the office for billing questions.

Do I need someone to help me afterward?

For surgery, plan to have someone drive you home and help you for a few days if needed. Your care team will explain exactly what to arrange.

Can I bring my saved list?

Yes. Tap the heart on anything that interests you, and we will add your list to your consultation request.
