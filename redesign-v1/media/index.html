Halo™ laser treatments

Over time, your skin can lose its youthful glow from sun exposure and natural aging. Halo targets the right depth to reduce years of damage and restore the luminous glow you had when you were younger.

Kybella™

Dr. Newman receiving a Kybella treatment. Kybella is the first and only FDA-approved nonsurgical treatment that contours and improves the appearance of fullness under the chin.

Microneedling

One of our patients receiving skin rejuvenation with microneedling and PRP, sometimes called a “vampire facial.” The goals are even tone and texture, smaller-looking pores, collagen production and more resilient skin.
